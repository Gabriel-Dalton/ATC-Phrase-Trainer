      CYVR

      Vancouver ATC Simulation

      Fly a full departure or arrival at Vancouver International — clearance delivery, ground, tower and terminal control, live on frequency. Listen to each transmission, key the mic, and read it back like a pro.

      • HOLD SPACE (or the mic button) to transmit — release to send
      • TYPE + ENTER works if you have no microphone
      • Readbacks are graded on the items that matter: runways, altitudes, headings, frequencies, squawks
      • Watch the radar — your aircraft actually flies what you read back
